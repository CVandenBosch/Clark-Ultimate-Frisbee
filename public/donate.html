Support WooTown Ultimate

We are the WooTown Killer Bees, Clark University's club Ultimate Frisbee team. We're coming off a delightful end to our incredible fall season where we won the Spirit Award at Lobster Pot — one of our favorite highlights of the year.

Spring Season

Heart of Clarkness (Men's) and Joan of Clark (Women & Non-Binary) are heading to NEO (Northeast Open) and Sectionals to represent Clark!

Our Goal

With a reduced university budget this year, we've set a goal of $1,200 to cover tournament registration fees and travel costs for the spring semester.

Why Tournaments Matter

Tournaments are where we build our skills, chemistry, and confidence as a team. They are the heart of our competitive season, but they are also our biggest expense.

Any amount helps! If you would like to be entered into a lottery to receive a WTKB-branded cup, add your name as a note when you make your donation.

Playing frisbee with the bees is (almost certainly) one of your loved one's favorite things about Clark. Thank you for helping us have the most fun, competitive, and community-filled season possible!
